About

Who this is, and why this archive exists.

I am Ankush Sarkar. Across the years I’ve moved between making things for an audience and quietly building with technology — sometimes in public, more often in private. This site pulls those parts of my life into a single place so they can be read as one story rather than scattered fragments.

“The archive is not a record of what I have done. It is a record of how one life kept company with its own interests.”

More of the story will be filled in over time. For now, consider this the opening pages — the binding before the chapters.
